About me

I'm Abraão Moreira, a full-stack developer in Pindamonhangaba, Brazil (remote, UTC-3). For more than five years I have built web systems, integrations, and automations that remove friction from everyday work.

Today I work in municipal government as an IT analyst and software developer, leading projects and integrations: I have migrated legacy systems to Next.js, implemented single sign-on with Keycloak and Active Directory, and coordinated work with several vendors. New systems ship with automated tests, and I mentor four interns.

Since 2020 I have developed MQL4 and MQL5 automations and decision-support tools for trading clients. Earlier I built ERP and management systems, a high-traffic front end, and public-sector software at SNPS Group, TIVIT, and Grupo Embras. I hold a B.Sc. in Science and Technology from UNIFESP.
